    // Manifesto

    Three rules we keep coming back to. They are not aspirational — they are how the work gets merged.

    1. 01

      Build in the open

      Every project here is public, from the first commit. Issues, roadmaps and the boring internal notes live where anyone can read them.

    2. 02

      Small tools, sharp edges

      One problem, solved completely. We would rather ship a focused tool that composes than a platform that swallows everything.

    3. 03

      Ship weekly, document daily

      Momentum over perfection. If a decision is not written down next to the code, it does not exist.

    // Fork, patch, ship

    The collective is 12 repositories and a small group of maintainers. Grab the CLI, take an open issue, and open a pull request — that is the whole application process.
